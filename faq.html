Orders & Shipping

How long does shipping take?
Domestic orders (Portugal & Spain) arrive in 1–3 business days. Rest of Europe takes 3–5 days, and international orders typically arrive within 5–10 business days. In-stock items ship within 24 hours.
Do you ship internationally?
Yes — we ship to over 25 countries. Shipping costs and delivery estimates are calculated at checkout based on your address.
Can I change or cancel my order after placing it?
You can modify or cancel an order within 2 hours of placing it, before it enters fulfillment. Contact us as soon as possible through the contact page and we'll do our best to help.
How do I track my order?
You'll receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. If you didn't get one, check your spam folder or reach out to us directly.

Repairs & Warranty

How does the repair process work?
Bring your device in-store or ship it to us. We run free diagnostics, send you a fixed-price quote, and only start work once you approve it. Most repairs are completed within 24 hours.
What does the 2-year warranty cover?
Every device we sell and every repair we perform is covered for two years against defects in parts and workmanship — no cost to you for eligible claims.
Do you repair devices you didn't sell?
Yes, we repair most laptop and desktop brands regardless of where they were purchased. Diagnostics are always free.

Products & Compatibility

Will this component work with my current setup?
Send us your current specs through the contact form or ask in-store — our team checks compatibility before you buy, at no charge.
Do you offer trade-in or upgrade credit?
Yes — bring in your old laptop or desktop for an instant trade-in quote toward any purchase in-store.
Can I get a custom-built desktop?
Absolutely. Tell us your budget and use case (gaming, editing, general use) and we'll configure a build, assemble it, and stress-test it before handover.

Returns & Refunds

What's your return policy?
Unopened items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Opened hardware can be returned within 14 days if it's in resellable condition, subject to inspection.
My order arrived damaged — what now?
Send us a photo of the damaged item through the contact form within 7 days of delivery, and we'll send a free replacement.
How long do refunds take to process?
Once approved, refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Still need a hand?

Our team replies within one business day, every day of the week.
